2 · Privacy

What happens to your data when you use this page

Information under Article 13 GDPR · last changed 18 October 2026

Controller

Inceptune OÜ, address as in the imprint above, hello@inceptune.com. No data protection officer has been appointed.

The file you drop in is not processed by us

This is the one claim worth checking rather than believing. Your file is read by code running in your own browser tab. It is not uploaded, not copied, and not transmitted — there is no server of ours that could receive it, which is also why no account exists and no cookie is set.

You do not have to take that on trust. Open your browser's developer tools, switch to the network panel, and drop a file in: no request leaves the page. The code that does the reading is inceptune.js, published in full for exactly this reason.

What the host logs

The page itself is delivered by GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. (San Francisco, USA). Delivering any web page means your IP address reaches the server that sends it. GitHub states this plainly in its own documentation:

„When a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, regardless of whether the visitor has signed into GitHub or not.“

Open point, stated rather than smoothed over

Two things are not settled here and we will not invent an answer. First, GitHub's current privacy statement does not say how long these logs are kept, so no retention period is given above. Second, whether GitHub acts as our processor or as an independent controller for logs it makes for its own security purposes is a legal classification we have not had reviewed. The disclosure above is deliberately the wider one: you are told who receives the data either way.

If you write to us

If you send mail to hello@inceptune.com, we process your address and the content of your message in order to answer it — Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message concerns a contract or its preparation, otherwise Article 6(1)(f), the legitimate interest in answering a question that was put to us. We keep such correspondence as long as the matter is open and thereafter as long as a statutory retention duty requires.

What there is none of

  • No cookies. None are set, so there is nothing to consent to and no banner.
  • No analytics, no pixel, no tag manager, no A/B tool, no session recording.
  • No account, no login, no newsletter, no profiling and no automated decision-making within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR.
  • No fonts, scripts or images fetched from a third party. Everything the page uses is either part of the page or already on your device.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Article 15), rectification (16), erasure (17), restriction of processing (18), data portability (20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (21). Write to hello@inceptune.com. Note the practical consequence of the paragraphs above: for a visit to this page we hold no data about you that we could hand over, correct or erase — the logs are GitHub's, not ours.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Article 77). The authority competent for us is the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate, Andmekaitse Inspektsioon (www.aki.ee). You may equally turn to the authority of your own place of residence.

3 · Terms

Terms of use

Last changed 18 October 2026

What is on offer today

Today there is exactly one thing: the free tier, which runs in your browser without an account and without payment. Nothing is sold here at present, so no contract for consideration is concluded by using this page.

Use

You may use the page for your own purposes, commercial ones included, and may keep and pass on the reports it produces — they are your output over your data. What is not granted is a licence to the source code: it is published so that the claim „no network request after load“ is checkable, not as a release for reuse. The precise wording, including what reading and quoting remains expressly permitted, is in LICENSE.

What this tool does and does not claim

The five counts are deterministic: the same file yields the same result, and every figure states how it was produced — counted, a ratio, your own input, or not determined. That is the whole claim.

It follows that the tool reports on the file you gave it, and on nothing else. It does not know whether your file is complete, current or representative, it does not advise, and a finding of „not determined“ is an honest statement about the data rather than a defect. Decisions you take on the basis of a report are yours.

Availability

The page is served as a static file by GitHub Pages. No availability is promised and none is owed; if the host is down, the page is down. Because the free tier needs no server of ours, a copy you have already loaded keeps working in that tab.

Premium, once it exists

The premium tier is not live. When it becomes available, billing will run through a merchant of record — a provider that is itself the seller towards you. That matters for your rights, so the provider will be named on this page before the first sale, and its buyer terms will then govern the purchase, including the statutory right of withdrawal for consumers and the VAT shown on your invoice.

The prices quoted on the front page — $12 per month, $120 per year — are stated net, excluding value added tax. The applicable tax is the one of your own country, it is added at checkout, and it is shown on the invoice issued by the merchant of record. There are no delivery charges, because there is nothing to deliver.

Open point — not yet decided

The merchant of record has not been chosen. Until it is, this page cannot name the withdrawal procedure, the refund rule or the governing buyer terms, and it will not guess at them. No payment can be taken before that is filled in.

Liability and applicable law

Estonian law applies. Where you are a consumer, the mandatory consumer protection of your country of residence is unaffected by that choice.
